# Dungeon Diagram

Reads room offsets and wave definitions from the tutorial level. Seven rooms share adjacent borders in a 13 by 13 cell diagram; the pond branches east. Native frame sprites are merged at shared corners, without black cropping masks, so their strokes form connected junctions. Number tabs remain readable above the borders.

The table follows the native numbered room order, 1 through 7. Room 5 is Waves; its icons come from the actual wave definitions.

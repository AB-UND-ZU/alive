# Quests

```widget
popup quest
```

Read [[unit:sign]] for nearby instructions. Approach a character when [[button:QUEST]] appears. Follow the marker, complete the target or bring the requested items, then return and claim the reward. A request may choose one of several targets; the dialog shows your current one.

[[key:Enter]] opens the quest dialog or confirms its selected action.

## Dungeon

The tutorial [[unit:sign]] leads through movement, conversations, keys, doors and combat. Read each [[unit:sign]] and follow its highlighted target to open the next route. Finishing the Dungeon leads to the Island.

## Fire Haven

| Character | Task | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[unit:fireChief]] | Bring [[item:3 golem]] | [[item:gold]]<br>[[item:10 xp]]<br>[[item:pickaxe wood]] |
| [[unit:fireChief]] | Bring [[unit:fireNomad]]'s [[item:schema]] | [[item:gold]]<br>[[item:3 xp]]<br>One accessory:<br>[[item:ring]]<br>or [[item:amulet]] |
| [[unit:fireChief]] | Defeat [[unit:wormBoss]] in its cave | [[item:75 coin]]<br>[[item:10 xp]]<br>[[item:hammer wood]]<br>[[item:letter]] |
| [[unit:fireChief]] | Build a boat and take the letter to [[unit:waterGuard]] in the center of glacier island | Explore glacier island and the ocean |
| [[unit:fireSmith]] | Defeat the requested enemies:<br>10 [[unit:goldPrism]]<br>or 8 [[unit:goldEye]]<br>or 6 [[unit:goldOrb]] | [[item:gold]]<br>[[item:5 xp]] |
| [[unit:fireDruid]] | Bring the requested supplies | [[item:gold]]<br>[[item:5 xp]] |

After the worm quest, use the rewarded [[item:hammer wood]] to choose a boat. Craft its [[item:1 plank]] and [[item:1 iron]], place a Plot in shallow water, then select Repair to finish it. The jetty guard steps aside when approached and returns to its post after you leave. Sail to the water guard in the glacier's center; the quest focuses on that guard.

[[unit:fireDruid]] requests one of: [[item:2 juice]], [[item:2 curry]], [[item:2 crystal]], [[item:2 mineral]], [[item:10 thorn]], [[item:5 potion iron hp]] or [[item:5 potion iron mp]].

## Belongings

After [Respawn](respawn.md), follow the grave marker to recover dropped items. A waypoint marker tracks a target; it is not a separate quest reward.

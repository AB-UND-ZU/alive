# Bosses

Bosses guard encounters with stronger attacks. Their stats and factions are listed below.

## Worm cave

After the earlier Fire Haven tasks, [[unit:fireChief]] asks you to defeat [[unit:wormBoss]]. Follow the quest focus to its cave. Bring [[item:pickaxe wood]] to open the way and [[item:sword gold]] for the fight. A single jetty segment reaches into the lake from the south.

Killing the small worm starts a short pause, then the cave closes before the earthquake. The opening shockwave destroys cave walls and lights the fire ring cell by cell as it approaches. After the opening wave finishes, the worm jumps from the lake into sand. Later jumps emerge and land on sand; the empty hole lingers briefly after the last segment goes underground, and the burrowed worm cannot be hit. The worm stops with its head exposed before shouting question marks on its first emergence or exclamation marks on entering rage. Its shout disappears before attacks begin. It shouts dots while dying. Watch for holes and flashing artillery targets. Reach the exposed head to interrupt its artillery; it eventually retreats even if you cannot reach it. Artillery fires last longer and may remain after the phase ends.

Jump attacks cycle between spaced disc pairs, dense pairs along the top of the flight, and a shorter continuous shield line following the outside of the jump arc. It begins beside the neck and its final disc lands with the tail, adjusting when the body shortens.

When health thresholds are crossed in flight, only the tail is lost. If it is still underground, the exposed body keeps emerging; fully exposed tails detach and stop. Its summon launches as soon as decay finishes, while the red vanish plays. While surfaced, the neck launches the summon without breaking the exposed body. A [[unit:wormTower]] appears when the red disc reaches a free nearby point on a single ring midway across the sand between the lake and cave edge, keeping some distance from the decayed segment. Iron towers stay anchored. During jumps they fire three consecutive pairs of straight shots in opposite directions, then pause. At the start of artillery, each tower launches two red circling discs that disappear on hitting a target. They are not replaced during that phase. Towers can still charge a slash when you approach. After at least two shots since their last slash, nearby players can trigger a four-tick iron slash. Towers finish a queued three-shot burst first. The slash deals one melee damage with knockback and no burning. Towers ignore fire, have 40 health and two armor, and drop one heart.

The worm has 750 health, three armor and minus two resistance, and remaining fires cannot hurt or burn it. At 25% health it stays in its lake rage phase until defeated. Destroy towers before their green bolts restore eight health each to the boss. Towers only heal during rage, with no bursts or slashes; they wait while its health is above 25%. The huge wave covers almost the whole cave; step into the lake to extinguish burning. Rage chooses a different attack each time from huge waves, four delayed gold blasts from the mouth center in shuffled cardinal directions, each dealing five magic damage with repeated knockback and no burning, and exactly three repeated red slashes with strong knockback. Both worm and tower slashes show the larger rage warning for four ticks; other attacks use the normal warning. Each new three-percent health threshold below 25% launches another tower summon from the neck, through the mouth, to a free point on the upper half of the tower ring; healing cannot repeat a threshold.

On defeat, the whole mouth and neck dissolve from red dots into squares. A golden [[unit:wormChest]] appears at the jetty tip with one fire element, five gold and ten iron health potions.

Return to [[unit:fireChief]] for the reward. His next task is to build a boat using the build menu, then sail toward glacier island and explore the ocean and other islands.

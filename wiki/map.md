# Minimap

Equip [[item:map]] to see discovered terrain and landmarks. The green marker shows your position. Each view below shows the same explored Island at a different zoom.

```widget
minimap
```

[[key:M]] Open the Minimap.

[[key:↑]] [[key:↓]] Change the map zoom.

[[key:Escape]] Close the Minimap.


Explore to reveal cells. A [[item:compass]] points toward its target. Walking past an edge brings you to the opposite side of the level. See [Light](light.md) for unexplored and remembered terrain.

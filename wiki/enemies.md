# Enemies

Enemies move and attack according to their behavior. Approach with a weapon equipped or use a spell from a distance.

## Bandit Camps

Two or three bandits guard a [[unit:goldChest]] on a ring of grass around the fire. The chest holds one [[item:letter]] in addition to its usual loot. The other corners can hold up to two more containers: a [[unit:box]], [[unit:pot]], [[unit:woodChest]] or [[unit:ironChest]].

The northernmost bandit keeps lookout. It whistles at rest and shows a question mark three cells away. Come within two cells or hit a bandit from a distance and the whole camp attacks. Wounded bandits return when they lose sight of you and chase again if they spot you. They turn back beyond 15 cells from the fire unless you stay within two cells of a bandit.

The [[unit:banditPriest]] heals wounded campmates, even after they return to the fire. Once everyone recovers, the lookout starts whistling again. A lone priest retreats near the fire. The [[unit:banditMage]] attacks from a distance with a wand. Get close and it can lay a freezing trap every [[data:4 s]]. You have [[data:1 s]] to get clear. The [[unit:banditKnight]] closes in and gives two ticks of warning before slashing. The [[unit:banditRogue]] switches from a freezing sword to a bow below half health.

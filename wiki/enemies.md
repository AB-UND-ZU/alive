# Enemies

Enemies move and attack according to their behavior. Approach with a weapon equipped or use a spell from a distance.

## Bandit Camps

Bandits guard a [[unit:goldChest]] containing a [[item:letter]]. The lookout whistles until the camp spots you. Priests heal, mages lay traps, knights slash and wounded rogues switch to bows.

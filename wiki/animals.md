# Animals

A [[unit:chick]] wanders, runs away from nearby heroes and follows dropped grain or worms. An idle chick can peck the grass, bush or soil under its feet. Each peck on planted soil has a 20% chance to dig up the seed and drop it. Each chick lays one [[item:egg]] at a time and can lay another once its egg is collected.

[[item:worm]] is carried as fishing bait. Fish are shown as their catch items; [[item:salmon]], [[item:tuna]], [[item:cod]] and [[item:pike]] can also swim in shallow water.

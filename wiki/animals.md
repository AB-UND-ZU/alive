# Animals

A fed [[unit:chick]] has a 0.3% chance to grow each world tick, eating all its grain and worms. Silver chicks become [[unit:hen]] and yellow chicks become [[unit:rooster]]. Two fed, healthy adults show hearts, meet, and lay an [[item:egg]]. One parent eats all its feed. Leave the egg on the ground to hatch.

A hurt rooster spots players within five cells. If a chase takes it beyond ten cells, it returns to where it spotted the player and resumes wandering. Feed it to calm it and restore its health. Hens also heal when fed. Healing uses all the grain and worms the bird carries.

Chicks dig up planted seeds on one in five pecks. Adults dig them up twice as often. Chicks drop one [[item:raw]] and adults drop two. Brew Raw with a stick to make [[item:meat]].

[[item:worm]] is carried as fishing bait. Fish are shown as their catch items; [[item:salmon]], [[item:tuna]], [[item:cod]] and [[item:pike]] can also swim in shallow water.

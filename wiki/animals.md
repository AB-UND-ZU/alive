# Animals

Drop grain or worms to feed a [[unit:chick]]. A fed chick can lay an [[item:egg]]. Leave the egg on the ground to hatch. Chicks peck to their right and dig up planted seeds on one in four pecks.

[[item:worm]] is carried as fishing bait. Fish are shown as their catch items; [[item:salmon]], [[item:tuna]], [[item:cod]] and [[item:pike]] can also swim in shallow water.

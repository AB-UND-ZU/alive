# Signs

Approach [[unit:sign]] and choose [[button:READ]]. Signs explain nearby controls, items and hazards. Scroll within a long sign to read the rest.

```widget
popup menu_sign spawn_sign potion_sign fruit_sign guide_sign trader_sign worm_sign credits_sign
```

[[key:Enter]] Read.

[[key:↑]] [[key:↓]] Scroll.

[[key:Escape]] Close.

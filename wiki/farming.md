# Farming

```widget
popup plant
```

Plant a supported item in watered soil. Growth varies between stages; snow delays it. A wood shovel clears soil, planted seeds, grass and bushes. Use a shovel with at least 2 Farming to dig up stumps, foundations and other building remains. Keep water near the soil or use a filled [[item:bucket iron water]].

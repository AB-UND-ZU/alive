# Island

```widget
play island
```

Explore the jungle, desert, glacier and ocean.

## Map

```widget
world-map
```

See [Minimap](map.md) for the explored map.

## Earth Town

```widget
earth-town-map
```

Visit the [[unit:earthChief]] to begin the town quests.

## Fire Haven

```widget
fire-haven-map
```

Visit the [[unit:fireChief]] in the desert. The jetty leads to the ocean.

See [Quests](quests.md#fire-haven) for the settlements' progression and [Trading](trading.md) for buying and selling.

## Glacier

```widget
glacier-village-map
```

Follow the wooden paths to the [[unit:waterChief]]. Bandits guard treasure in the surrounding snow.

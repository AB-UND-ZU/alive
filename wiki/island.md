# Island

```widget
play island
```

Explore the jungle, desert, glacier and ocean. The playable preview and town cutouts use seed **2026**. Settlements and shorelines vary with the seed in normal games.

## Map

```widget
world-map
```

This schematic shows the Island's main regions and landmarks. The [Minimap](map.md) page keeps its separate explored-world examples.

## Earth Town

```widget
earth-town-map
```

Earth Town has wooden buildings, traders, workshops and its chief. Talk to residents when the interaction prompt appears. Some services unlock as town quests progress. Guards watch the entrances. The view reveals all buildings' contents at once; normal play reveals interiors as you enter them.

## Fire Haven

```widget
fire-haven-map
```

Fire Haven is the desert settlement, with stone buildings, workshops, guards and the Fire Chief. The cutout includes the jetty and its harbor guard. Visit the chief for the town's story and use the workstations to prepare your equipment. Buildings are shown from inside to reveal their contents.

See [Quests](quests.md#fire-haven) for the settlements' progression and [Trading](shops.md) for buying and selling.

## Glacier

Sail to the glacier and meet the [[unit:waterChief]] inside the chief’s igloo. A [[unit:waterGuard]] defends each side of the entrance. [[unit:diamondPrism]], [[unit:diamondEye]] and [[unit:diamondOrb]] roam the snow. Mine through the mountain walls to uncover [[item:iron]] and occasional [[item:gold]] deposits. Each [bandit camp](enemies.md#bandit-camps) has a campfire and a [[unit:goldChest]].

A rock fence surrounds the rubble clearing. Follow the wooden paths to the water trader, smith, druid and chief. The three workshops have a workbench, anvil and kettle respectively.

The glacier additions come with newly generated islands. Loading an older island preserves its original terrain and inhabitants.

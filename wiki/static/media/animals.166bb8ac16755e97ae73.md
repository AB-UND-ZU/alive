# Animals

A fed [[unit:chick]] has a 0.3% chance to grow each world tick, eating all its grain and worms. Silver chicks become [[unit:hen]] and yellow chicks become [[unit:rooster]]. Two fed, healthy adults show hearts, meet, and lay an [[item:egg]]. One parent eats all its feed. Leave the egg on the ground to hatch.

A hurt rooster spots players within five cells. If a chase takes it beyond ten cells, it returns to where it spotted the player and resumes wandering. Feed it to calm it and restore its health. Hens also heal when fed. Healing uses all the grain and worms the bird carries.

Chicks dig up planted seeds on one in five pecks. Adults dig them up twice as often. Chicks drop one [[item:raw]] and adults drop two. Brew Raw with a stick to make [[item:meat]].

[[item:worm]] is carried as fishing bait. Fish are shown as their catch items; [[item:salmon]], [[item:tuna]], [[item:cod]] and [[item:pike]] can also swim in shallow water.

## Pet

A wild pet walks the streets of Earth Town. Stand nearby to see which fish it wants, [[item:salmon]] or [[item:pike]], and how many it still needs. Drop the requested fish from your Bag. The pet picks it up and collects its own XP dots. After three fish, its health bar turns green and it follows you. At each new level, green rings appear, followed by the stat gains and the new skill’s icon.

Pets start with 1 Haste and 2 Resist before personality bonuses. A scratch deals 1 base damage. Levels 2 and 3 each add 1 Power. Level 2 also adds 1 Haste. A pet with Aura grants its owner two Totem bonuses within two cells, chosen from Power, Wisdom, Armor and Resist.

A wild pet starts without skills. It learns one skill at taming and another at each level after that. Level 1 adds either Tuna or Cod. Level 2 adds a prepared food or gold flask from the table. Level 3 adds another favourite it has yet to learn. Every favourite gives double its usual stats, whether the pet asks for it or not.

Collect carries up to four item types per trip. It stacks matching items as in your Bag and leaves each stack on a free cell near you, starting in front. Any tamed pet can collect spare hearts and mana during a fight. It eats a heart when hurt and carries the rest. When you need them, it offers one at a time. Heal earns a healing hit after each kill. Mana earns 1 MP every second kill. Both can be saved until you need them.

Drop a [[item:stick]] to play fetch with a pet. When your companion asks for a stick, it circles you twice, returns it and earns 1 XP. If kept waiting, it may briefly refuse to follow.

Even a wild pet may occasionally scratch a chick beside it. A wild pet flees when hit and recovers health after a delay. A lethal hit kills it and drops one Air element.

Your companion rests at a fountain when hurt, avoids fires, and chases fairies even without Attack. It wakes if you travel too far away. It waits outside buildings in dry weather, follows you indoors during rain, and rides with you by boat. A thirsty pet keeps asking for water until you drop a filled [[item:bucket iron water]]. It leaves the empty bucket in front of you. A pet with a yellow halo shows its favourites when you stand beside it. Drop any one to revive it at full health.

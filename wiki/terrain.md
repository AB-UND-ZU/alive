# Terrain

Ground changes movement and what can be built or harvested. Deep water blocks walking away from shore.

Fire spells clear snow and melt the ice beneath it. Melting snow gives off a puff of smoke. A lit campfire melts one cell every three world ticks and keeps snow from settling within the same area. Its warmth reaches three cells above and below the fire and five cells to either side. The top and bottom rows are each three cells wide. Snow and ice spare cells occupied by the hero, an NPC or an opaque wall. Fish can flounder when their water freezes. You can bump snow to uncover it while holding a tool.

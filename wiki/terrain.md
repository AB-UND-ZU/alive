# Terrain

Ground changes movement and what can be built or harvested. Deep water blocks walking away from shore.

Fire spells clear snow and melt the ice beneath it. Melting snow gives off a puff of smoke. A lit campfire melts one cell every three world ticks and keeps snow from settling within the same area. Its warmth reaches three cells above and below the fire and five cells to either side. The top and bottom rows are each three cells wide. Falling snow spares occupied cells. A freezing spell also freezes water under a unit. Fish can flounder when their water freezes. Bump snow with any tool to clear it. Paths add 3 haste, sand removes 2, and water or the inside of a tornado removes 6.

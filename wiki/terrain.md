# Terrain

Ground changes movement and what can be built or harvested. Deep water blocks walking away from shore.

Fire spells clear snow and melt the ice beneath it. A lit campfire melts one nearby cell every three world ticks and keeps fresh snow from settling within a radius of five. Snow and ice spare cells occupied by the hero, an NPC or an opaque wall. Fish can flounder when their water freezes. You can bump snow to uncover it while holding a tool.
